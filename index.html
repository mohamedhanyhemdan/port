About me

I’m Mohamed, a video editor who turns raw footage into short, clear videos. Most of my work is educational and promotional content for Land Tricks and STEAM Academy, from kids’ coding classes and 3D printing workshops to talks with teachers.

I edit mainly in Premiere Pro and CapCut, add motion and titles in After Effects, and grade every project so it looks clean on a phone screen. I also work comfortably with Arabic subtitles and text animation.
